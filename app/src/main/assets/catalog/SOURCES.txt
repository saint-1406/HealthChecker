USDA FoodData Central data are in the public domain (CC0).
Source ids are preserved as usda:<fdcId>. Values describe average foods, not individual samples.
Russian names and Azerbaijani aliases are editorial translations. Plain yogurt is only an approximate search match for qatıq, not a measured local brand.
Portion masses are source estimates unless described as a household estimate. A glass of water is an editable 250 ml household estimate.
Open Food Facts was unavailable (HTTP 503); no OFF records are bundled in this build.
Regional brands and Azerbaijani recipes are not claimed as covered. Use a custom food from the product label when the precise product is absent.
